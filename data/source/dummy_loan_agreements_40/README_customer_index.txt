DATASET ASSUMPTIONS
- All borrowers, lender, people, addresses, income and revenue figures are invented.
- Currency: USD. Principal amounts range from $2,000 to $100,000,000.
- Eight households, eight microbusinesses, eight small businesses,
  eight midsize businesses and eight corporations.
- Size labels are illustrative dataset groupings, not statutory definitions.
- All loans are single-draw, fixed-rate and fully amortizing, funded 15 Sep 2026.
- Households, microbusinesses and small businesses repay monthly.
- Midsize businesses and corporations repay quarterly.
- Rates are arbitrary example nominal rates, not current market quotes or APR disclosures.
- Period interest = opening principal x nominal annual rate / payments per year.
- Interest is rounded to cents each period using half-up rounding.
- The final installment is adjusted to clear the remaining principal.
- No fees, payment holidays, balloon amounts or overdue-interest calculations are included.
- Printed due dates are not adjusted for weekends or holidays.
- Every document is a non-operative specimen and is deliberately unsigned.
- No real jurisdiction, legal compliance determination or actual transaction is represented.
- No real IDs, bank details, signatures or functioning email contacts are included.
